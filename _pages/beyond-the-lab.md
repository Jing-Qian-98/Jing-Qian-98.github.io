<p class="lede">Outside the lab, I love running and hiking in Colorado and beyond. Here are a few of my 2026 milestones.</p>
<h2>Running</h2>
<h3>BOLDERBoulder 10K</h3>
<p class="when">Memorial Day · May 25, 2026 · Boulder, CO</p>
<p>I started running in January 2026, and the BOLDERBoulder was the first time I had ever run a full 10K.</p>
<p>At the shakeout run before the race, I got to meet <strong>Coach Bennett</strong>, whose voice had been in my headphones on so many guided runs, and <strong>Conner Mantz</strong>! It was such a fun way to kick off race weekend.</p>

<h2>Hiking</h2>
<h3>Bryce Canyon &amp; Angels Landing</h3>
<p class="when">March 2026 · Utah</p>
<p>After our Extravehicular Activity (EVA) simulations at the <strong>Mars Desert Research Station</strong> (Hanksville, UT) for ASEN 5226 (Medicine in Space and Surface Environments), I went to Bryce Canyon and Angels Landing in Zion with my MDRS friends.</p>
<p>I am genuinely afraid of heights (haha), so I’m very grateful to my friends for encouraging me the whole way.</p>
